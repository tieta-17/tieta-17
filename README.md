# Hi, I'm An

I'm a third-year undergraduate student at the University of Virginia, double majoring in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

I'm passionate about hardware design and development, with particular interests in FPGA/RTL design, computer architecture, and embedded systems programming. Through coursework, research, and personal projects, I've gained hands-on experience working with digital logic, microcontrollers, and hardware-software integration.

I particularly enjoy understanding how systems operate at a low level, from designing digital hardware in Verilog to writing software that interacts directly with physical devices. One of my favorite projects has been developing a custom 32-bit RISC-V-inspired system-on-chip (SoC) on an FPGA, which has allowed me to explore the intersection of computer architecture, digital design, and embedded programming.

I'm always looking to expand my technical knowledge, tackle new engineering challenges, and gain experience developing real-world hardware systems.

## Projects
I'm currently working on [ANIMA](https://github.com/Rkycodes/ANIMA). 

I've developed an [FPGA based System on a Chip](https://github.com/tieta-17/tang-nano-9k-SOC) on a Gowin FPGA (Tang Nano 9K) equipped with memory mapped GPIO and UART, powered by a CPU running on my own custom RISC-V Inspired ISA.

To expand on the FPGA SOC, I am currently learning AXI and planning to integrate it into the current SOC. I also intend to move the SoC onto a Basys 3 Artix FPGA Development Board, utilizing the Vivado Toolkit as well.
